Connect or reconnect

Ledger Local MCP is a private, single-user app. It is connected to QuickBooks Online by its owner, who signs in through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 authorisation and stores the resulting tokens on their own computer.

To reconnect after access has expired or been revoked, the owner authorises the app again with Intuit and replaces the locally stored tokens. There is no self-service connection for other QuickBooks Online customers.
