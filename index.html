Ledger Local MCP

A private, single-user integration with QuickBooks Online.

Ledger Local MCP is a personal tool used by its owner to read accounting data from the owner's own QuickBooks Online company. It runs entirely on the owner's computer as a local Model Context Protocol (MCP) server, so the owner's AI assistant can look up records and reports and, at the owner's request, create or update records, for example to prepare and check data before lodging a Business Activity Statement.

The integration is configured so that it cannot delete data in QuickBooks Online.

This app is not offered to the public and is not available for other QuickBooks Online customers to install.
