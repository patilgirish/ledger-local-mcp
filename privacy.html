Privacy Policy

Ledger Local MCP · Effective 26 November 2026

Who uses this app

Ledger Local MCP is a personal, single-user tool. Its only user is its owner, connecting to the owner's own QuickBooks Online company. It is not offered to other people or businesses.

What data it accesses

With the owner's authorisation through Intuit's OAuth 2.0 sign-in, the app reads accounting data from the connected QuickBooks Online company. This can include company details, customers, suppliers, employees, items, transactions, tax codes and financial reports. The app is configured for read-only use and does not create, change or delete QuickBooks Online data.

Where data is processed and stored

  • The app runs only on the owner's own computer. There is no server operated for this app, and no QuickBooks data is stored on any website, including this one.
  • OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in a local configuration file on the owner's computer, readable only by the owner's user account.
  • QuickBooks data is fetched on demand and is not kept in a separate database by the app.
  • When the owner asks their AI assistant a question, the data returned by the app is passed to the AI assistant the owner has chosen, and is handled under that provider's terms and privacy policy.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone for marketing or any other purpose. Nothing is sent anywhere except between the owner's computer, Intuit's QuickBooks Online API and the owner's chosen AI assistant, as described above.

Cookies and analytics

This website and the app do not use cookies, tracking or analytics.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the app in QuickBooks Online. The owner can delete the locally stored tokens and any locally saved outputs at any time.

Security

Connections to QuickBooks Online use HTTPS. Credentials are kept out of source control and stored with file permissions limited to the owner.

Changes

Any change to this policy will be published on this page with a new effective date.
